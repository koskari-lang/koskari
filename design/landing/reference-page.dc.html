Koskari is a stackless Lisp that lives next to Python — real continuations, ambient async, and an indentation mode that frees you from the parenthesis curse. Come have fun with programming again.

Why it's fun
⇆

Lives with Python

Import any module, pass objects both ways. Not a transpiler — a real stackless interpreter running side by side with CPython.

( )

Beyond the parens

A novel indentation layout lets you write Lisp's power without drowning in brackets. Drop back into S-expressions whenever you like.

↻

Continuations, really

Stackless to the core: first-class continuations, coroutines, and control flow you can pick up and hold in your hand.

∿

Ambiently async

Concurrency without the colored functions. Everything can await, nothing has to. The runtime just gets out of your way.

See it for yourself

A point, and its neighbors

That @dataclass comes straight from Python's standard library — Koskari just uses it. Notice the layout: no wall of closing parens in sight.
