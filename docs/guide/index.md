# Guide

Shared concept spine for both tutorial on-ramps. Each chapter is a quick
orientation — enough to decide whether a concept matters for your use case now,
or is something to revisit later. When you want the exact rules, a chapter
points you to the authoritative [concepts](../reference/concepts/index.md)
pages. Chapters build one idea at a time; later chapters assume earlier ones.

Start with [Model of evaluation](model-of-evaluation.md) after the
[beginner track](../start/beginner/index.md) or the
[from-Python track](../start/from-python/index.md).
